## Mathematical and editorial boundaries

The qubit and phase experiments use ideal pure states and exact probabilities. They do not simulate shots, noise or model training. The workflow interaction is explicitly a conceptual walkthrough.

The Bloch graphic omits global phase and distinguishes coordinates from amplitudes. The phase graphic includes P(φ), not just an unexplained sign change. H is a unitary transformation followed by a separate measurement.

The cat diagram is an idealized thought experiment, not evidence that an ordinary unopened box is a coherent superposition. Bell correlations are contrasted with a classical mixture; identical Z statistics alone do not establish entanglement. No controllable faster-than-light message is implied.

The opening draws on the instructor's new introductory transcript and Introduction.pptx, with newly authored supporting explanations and calculations. Classroom dialogue and raw source files are not uploaded. Vendor counts, dated forecasts and broad speedup claims from the source material were not recycled into the enduring introduction.
