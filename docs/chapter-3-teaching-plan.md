# Chapter 3 teaching plan and source map

## Reading sequence

Chapter overview and seven lessons: complex numbers; real dot products/norms/projections; bras and complex inner products; outer products/projectors; matrices/adjoints; unitaries; tensor products. Each lesson has an original editable SVG, an activity, two concept checks with feedback for every choice, and three independent practice problems with worked solutions. Six cumulative problems appear in the chapter overview. The Colab companion reproduces all seven topics with NumPy and includes assertions and cumulative calculations.

## Mathematical and teaching sources

Primary material: Lecture Slides - 1 to 7.pdf pages 4, 8–10, 14–17, 21–22, 28–31, 41–45. Additional worked context: pages 18, 23–27, 35–38. The August 8 transcript (2Quantum Machine Learning (S1-26_AIMLZG545).docx), particularly around 14:45 and the complex arithmetic discussion, informs the move from a complex-plane picture to conjugation and squared magnitude. Source practice Q1–5, Q17 and Q19 support normalization, overlap and projector exercises. Raw dialogue and participant details are excluded.

Added exposition fills prerequisites: signed real projection; dot product versus projection length; zero normalization; general conjugate-linearity; inner/outer/tensor dimensions; a normalized versus raw outer-product projector; all-input unitarity versus one-input checks. Geometric real-plane drawings are explicitly bounded. Full measurement, gate catalogues and entanglement theory remain in their subsequent chapters.
