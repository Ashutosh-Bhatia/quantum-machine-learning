Locate the quantum component

In a variational model, an encoding circuit maps data to a state, trainable gates transform it, and a measurement statistic defines a prediction. In a quantum-kernel model, state preparation and overlap estimation supply a similarity matrix used by a classical learner. Data, loss, optimization and evaluation remain part of the full procedure.

\[k_Q(x,z)=|\langle\phi(x)|\phi(z)\rangle|^2.\]

This common fidelity kernel is real and nonnegative and is a PSD kernel for ideal normalized state features. Other quantum kernel definitions exist. Its associated feature geometry is not simply the original complex state amplitude vector with an ordinary real dot product.
