How do separate qubits become one joint quantum system, and what changes when their state cannot be factored? This chapter develops the mathematics and experiments behind entanglement.

The convention used throughout

Write |AB⟩, with basis order 00, 01, 10, 11. In the Qiskit notebook A=q1 and B=q0. Every activity states which subsystem is transformed or measured. Begin with a joint column; interpret probabilities only after declaring the measurement.
