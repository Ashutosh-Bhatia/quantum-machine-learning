Chapter challenge

What a complete answer should explain

Compare declared degrees and penalties within the same validation protocol, including preprocessing inside folds. Inspect validation error and its variation, not training fit alone. Evaluate the selected procedure on the held-out set after choices. Further design based on that result requires a new independent evaluation rather than another claim using the same test data.
